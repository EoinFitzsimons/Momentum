Presentation Script - Teleprompter Version (3 minute slot)

Notes: This is a teleprompter-style script. Each slide has one paragraph to read aloud, an exact timing, an emphasis cue, and a single-line takeaway to repeat aloud.

Slide 1 - Title (20 seconds)
Motorsport management games are niche and often single‑discipline or shallow in simulation. Inspired by modding work on car‑centric titles and by low‑fi, vehicle‑focused play, the project aims are: broader coverage across disciplines, depth in management systems (contracts, staff, budgets, vehicle setup), and practical deliverability in a single repository with lightweight dependencies.

Slide 2 - The Past - What Compares? (40 seconds)
Read: "Existing titles deliver strong presentation and race visuals, but often rely on linear progression and scripted outcomes. My starting question was how to create systemic depth while keeping simulations performant and reproducible. Audits and playtests revealed brittle data imports and fragile demo flows, which set the early priorities for automated validation and a simple, testable quick-race path."
Emphasis: stress "systemic depth", "performant and reproducible", and "automated validation". Pause 0.75 seconds after the sentence with "priorities".
Takeaway: "Problem and priorities." Pause 1 second.

Slide 3 - The Present - The MVP (50 seconds)
Read: "The current MVP focuses on a playable Quick Race and telemetry capture for analysis. Monte Carlo simulation was used earlier in the project as a research tool to stress-test assumptions and identify failure modes, and is not the primary demo path. To reach this MVP we archived legacy code, added focused playability tests, and implemented import and verification tooling for core data. Season progression, save and load, and higher-fidelity offline simulations remain future work."
Emphasis: highlight "playable Quick Race" and "Monte Carlo used as research tool". Pause 0.5 seconds between clauses.
Takeaway: "Working, focused MVP." Pause 1 second.

Slide 4 - The Future - What Will Come? (30 seconds)
Read: "Next steps are precise and actionable: complete the deterministic game loop, integrate seeded tests into continuous integration, optimise simulation hotspots and add optional multiprocessing, and produce a packaged demo build. Throughout the project we identified challenging aspects of the process - data integrity, demo fragility, and performance trade-offs - and documented conceptual diagrams and alternative solutions that guided each decision. These artifacts will inform the CI and packaging work to reduce fragility and increase reproducibility."
Emphasis: stress "conceptual diagrams and alternative solutions" and "reduce fragility". Pause 0.5 seconds after each major item.
Takeaway: "Roadmap informed by design artifacts." Pause 1 second.

Slide 5 - Demo and Next Steps (25 seconds)
Read: "I will run a short Quick Race to show the HUD and the telemetry summary. If a live run is not possible, I will show a recorded trace and walk through the same telemetry. The single ask is two weeks focused time for CI and packaging to produce reproducible nightly builds and a stable demo artifact."
Emphasis: stress "single ask" and the duration "two weeks". Pause 1 second after the ask and repeat the ask once more in shorter form.
Takeaway: "One clear ask." Pause 1 second.

Closing line (5 seconds)
Read: "Thank you. I am ready for questions."
Cue: smile and pause for questions.

Delivery and rehearsal guidance

- Read each paragraph at a steady pace; use the specified pauses as breathing and emphasis points.
- If time runs long: remove the second sentence from Slide 3 and Slide 2 first. Practice until each slide fits the allotted time.
- Keep language direct: one headline, one paragraph, one takeaway per slide.

Additional presenter cues (for practice)

- Before Slide 2: take a 1.5 second breath, set eye contact, then read.
- Before Slide 3: emphasise the words listed in emphasis above and slow slightly on the key metric words.
