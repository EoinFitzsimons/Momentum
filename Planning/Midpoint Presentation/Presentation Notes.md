Examiners can only mark what they are given evidence of. Use this time to highlight your best work and provide clear evidence for the rubric.

Work to the rubric (available on Moodle).

Total presentation and demonstration time: maximum 5 minutes.

Introduction: introduce yourself, your course and specialisation, and the project title. This contributes to the communication section of the rubric.

Project overview: what the project is, who it is for, what problem it addresses, and how it differs from existing applications or research.

Technologies and tools: list the technologies and tools used to build the application, run analyses, and present results.

Project plan and progress: summarise completed work, remaining tasks, priorities, key risks, and mitigation strategies.

Demonstration (software/prototype): show working functionality of the prototype to provide a visual understanding of the finished application.

Presentation tips:

Speak clearly; do not rush.
Keep slides to 5 or fewer to cover everything within 5 minutes.
Do not overload slides with text; use slides to support your spoken narrative.
Ensure slides are error-free and presented in a logical order.
