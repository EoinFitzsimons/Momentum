# Competitor IA Notes — Momentum (Midpoint Focus)

Brief scan of comparable management sims to inform labels, navigation, and hierarchy.

Targets reviewed (patterns only):
- Motorsport Manager / F1 Manager
- Football Manager (navigation/terminology cues)

Key IA patterns observed
1) Left sidebar for dense management areas; top bar for global context (club/team name, finances, date).
2) Clear top-level groups: Squad/Drivers, Staff, Tactics/Setup, Schedule/Calendar, Finances, Training/Development.
3) Primary CTA on landing: Continue/Advance (race day) or Play/Start Match equivalent.
4) Persistent status cards on dashboard: finances, morale/form, next fixture/location.
5) Lists always sortable/filterable; comparison overlays for people/entities.
6) Standings/Results always reachable in 1–2 clicks from main nav.
7) Terminology consistency: Hire/Sign/Contract; Upgrade/Develop; Schedule/Calendar.

Recommendations to apply for Momentum IA
- Use a left sidebar with top-level: Dashboard, Drivers, Garage, Race/Calendar, Standings/Results, Reports (adv), Store, Settings.
- Keep a prominent "Start/Enter Race" CTA on Dashboard (mirrors "Continue/Advance").
- Always show next race tile (location/date) on Dashboard; link directly to Race Setup.
- Ensure driver/staff lists have filters and sorting; add a comparison entry point (even if basic) for advanced users.
- Use consistent verbs: Hire (not mix with Contract), Upgrade, Enter Race, View Standings.
- Surface standings/results in the main nav (not buried under Reports).
